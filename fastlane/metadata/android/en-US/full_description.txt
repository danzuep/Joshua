A simple, free and open source Bible reader for Android with a minimalist design.

<ul>
<li>Read offline: translations are downloaded once from eBible.org and stored on your device</li>
<li>Compare translations side by side</li>
<li>Search every verse</li>
<li>Keep notes, bookmarks and highlights</li>
<li>Copy or share verses with consecutive verses consolidated</li>
<li>Light and dark themes</li>
<li>Tiny install, no ads, no tracking, no analytics</li>
</ul>

Translations come from eBible.org as USFM files, including the public domain World English Bible (WEB). You can also add your own download sites.
