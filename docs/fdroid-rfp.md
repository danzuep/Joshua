### Summary:

Free, offline Bible reader with translation comparison, search and notes

### Description:

A simple, free and open source Bible reader for Android with a minimalist design. Translations are downloaded once from eBible.org as USFM files and then read offline. The World English Bible, a public domain translation, is the default. You can compare translations side by side, search every verse, keep notes, bookmarks and highlights, and copy or share verses. Light and dark themes are included. The app has no ads, tracking or analytics.

The first F-Droid release is v0.27.1. The build recipe is in [`docs/com.github.danzuep.yeshua.android.yml`](https://github.com/danzuep/eBible/blob/main/docs/com.github.danzuep.yeshua.android.yml) and the store listing is in [`fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/`](https://github.com/danzuep/eBible/tree/main/fastlane/metadata/android/en-US).
